Why Odoo is not working for the kitchen
  • It is too complicated for the team. Stock is booked into Studio or Cause and then has to be moved to the Kitchen.
  • So nobody has live control of what is actually on the shelf.
  • The recipes have grown messy: 127 recipes in Odoo, 23 of them with no ingredients, and drinks and meals split into separate eat-in and take-away copies (Cup O Queen alone is 16 copies).
Loyverse: the good and the not so good

The good

  • Simple screens the team can learn quickly.
  • A recipe takes its ingredients off stock when it is sold.
  • Batches: make a batch and the ingredients come off, the batch goes on.
  • Low stock fills the order to the supplier for you.
  • It costs little to run.

The not so good

  • Only one layer of choices. As you found, a dish cannot offer sourdough or health bread, or regular or almond milk, and take off the right one.
  • No units on recipe lines: it says 0.150 of a 1 kg pack, not 150 g.
  • A batch shows no cost at all until it has been made once, so a new dish shows the wrong profit.
  • Take-away packaging is either always counted or never.
  • One supplier per item.
  • The Badger app would still need its own menu, times and rules.
How we are doing it
  • One recipe list for the Kitchen.
  • Each recipe has its ingredients, its take-away extras (the box, cup or lid, taken off stock only when it is ordered as take-away) and its choices (bread, milk, beans, shots, sauce), where the choice decides which ingredient comes off.
  • The cost is worked out from the ingredients, never typed in.
  • Once your recipes are final we bring them in, and then link each ingredient to its stock item.
